What it does

Everything a scanner app should do — minus the upload.

Built for the 40 MB scans that clog your inbox. Each file is validated before anything is written, and your original is never touched.

🎚️

Four honest modes

Ultra compress retries at 96, 80, 64 and 48 dpi until the file is under 500 KB. Maximum downsamples to 150 dpi at quality 60, Balanced uses 200 dpi at 78, and High quality keeps 300 dpi images sharp. A lossless pass rewrites structure only.

🩶

Grayscale mode

One switch turns every embedded image gray — the quickest win for text scans that carry colour nobody needs.

🧾

Real-world images

CMYK, Indexed and soft-masked images are decoded properly, so scans that other tools skip actually get smaller.

📚

Batch with a real cancel

Queue as many PDFs as you like. Watch per-file progress, then hit Cancel straight from the notification without reopening the app.

📐

Resize pages

A4, A5, Letter, Legal or a custom size in mm, cm or inches, with fit, fill or stretch — applied to every page or just the range you name. Custom sizes can lock to the source page's aspect ratio, so changing width keeps height in proportion.

📤

Preview before you save

Page-by-page preview with pinch zoom, plus a My files history of everything you have compressed or resized. Share a PDF in from any app and it still lands in the folder you pick.

How it works

Three taps, no round trip.

  1. 1

    Add PDFs

    Use the system picker, or share files straight from your file manager, mail or chat app.

  2. 2

    Pick a preset

    Ultra compress, Maximum, Balanced or High quality — plus a lossless pass and grayscale under advanced settings.

  3. 3

    Start

    Choose an output folder and go. Progress lives in the notification, and results survive screen rotation.

Privacy

No internet permission.
Not now, not ever.

The merged Android manifest has no INTERNET permission, so the app physically cannot upload your documents. Jobs are stored in a local database and finished work is cleaned up on its own.

  • No accounts, ads or analytics SDKs
  • Work continues in the background with a visible, cancelable notification
  • Output goes only to the folder you choose; sources are never overwritten

Questions

The short answers.

How do I install the app?

Grab the signed release build: DOXILUME-0.1.0.apk (15 MB, Android 8.0+). Open the download on your phone, allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks, and tap Install. Play Protect may offer to scan the file first — that is normal for an app that doesn’t come from the Play Store. If you installed an earlier 0.1.0 build it used a placeholder package name (com.example.doxilume); uninstall it first, because Android treats the renamed package (com.doxilume.app) as a separate app and its history does not carry over.

Does compression ever make a file bigger?

No. If the recompressed copy isn’t smaller, nothing is written and the file is reported as “No gain”. Resizing is the one exception — it publishes a new page size even when the file grows.

What happens if I close the app mid-batch?

The job is a WorkManager task recorded in a local database. It keeps running, shows progress in the notification, and is restored when you come back — even after a force stop.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. Files are read and written through the Storage Access Framework, so no storage permission is needed.

Can I build it myself instead?

This repository hosts the landing page and the signed APK; the Android project itself is not published here. The APK on this page is a signed release build targeting Android 16, and the app’s privacy policy describes exactly what it does with your files.

Where is the privacy policy?

The full policy lives at privacy.html and is also available inside the app under Privacy policy on the home screen, so it reads without a connection.

Why do scanned PDFs sometimes barely shrink?

Some scans are already JPEG-compressed at low resolution. The lossless pass only rewrites structure, and Maximum can’t beat a stream that is already efficient — the app tells you instead of pretending.
