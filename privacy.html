Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · applies to the DOXILUME Android app (version 0.1.0 and later) and this website.

DOXILUME is an offline PDF compressor and page resizer. It is built so that your documents never need to leave your phone, and this policy explains exactly what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

1. Nothing leaves your device

The DOXILUME app does not request the INTERNET permission. This is enforced in the shipped app's manifest, so the app cannot upload, sync, back up or transmit a document to us or to anyone else. All compression, resizing and image re-encoding happens locally on your phone.

2. What the app accesses

  • PDFs you pick through the Android system file picker, or send to DOXILUME with Share / Open with.
  • The output folder you choose, written through the Storage Access Framework (A4 / Letter resizing, compressed copies).
  • Shared or opened files are copied into the app's private storage so a batch can finish even if the source app closes. Those copies are deleted when the app cleans up its temporary files.

3. What is stored on your device

  • A local job list: file names, sizes, status, presets and results.
  • Your settings: compression preset, page range, grayscale choice and output folder.
  • Temporary imports used while a job runs.

This data lives in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted, never backed up to a server, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Finished jobs are pruned automatically and stale temporary imports are cleaned up on launch.

4. What is never collected

DOXILUME collects no personal data and shares none. There are no accounts, no names, email addresses or phone numbers, no location, contacts or calendar access, no advertising or device identifiers, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Sharing is your choice

A finished PDF leaves the app only when you tap Share or Open and pick a destination. That action is handled by Android and the app you choose. Once a file is shared, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

6. Permissions and why they exist

  • Notifications — show batch progress and the Cancel action.
  • Foreground service (data sync) — keep a user-started batch alive while that notification is visible.
  • Wake lock, receive boot completed — supplied by WorkManager so queued jobs can resume.
  • No internet or network-state permission — removed from the merged manifest; every job is local.
  • No storage permission — files are read and written only through the Storage Access Framework.

7. Children

DOXILUME is a document utility with no accounts and no data collection. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour or the law requires a change, this page is updated and the effective date above changes. The same policy is available inside the app under Privacy policy on the home screen, so it can be read without a connection.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or how DOXILUME handles files can be raised in the project's issue tracker: github.com/Aashupokemon/DOXILUME/issues.
